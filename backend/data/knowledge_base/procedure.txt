# Blood Donation Procedure

The whole blood donation process is simple, safe, and typically takes about 45 minutes to an hour from start to finish, though the actual blood drawing only takes 8-10 minutes.

Step 1: Registration
You will sign in, show ID, and read important information about donating.

Step 2: Mini-Physical and Screening
A medical professional will check your temperature, blood pressure, pulse, and hemoglobin levels (via a quick finger prick). You will also answer a confidential questionnaire about your medical history and lifestyle.

Step 3: The Donation
You'll recline on a comfortable chair. A phlebotomist will clean your arm and insert a sterile, single-use needle. About 1 pint (roughly 450-500ml) of blood will be collected. 

Step 4: Rest and Refreshments
After donating, you will rest in the observation area for 10-15 minutes. You will be provided with juice and snacks to help replenish your blood sugar and fluids.

Post-Donation Care:
Drink extra fluids for the next 24 hours. Avoid strenuous physical activity or heavy lifting for the rest of the day. If you feel dizzy, lie down with your feet elevated until the feeling passes.
