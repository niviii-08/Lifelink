# Blood Donation Eligibility

To ensure the safety of both the donor and the recipient, blood donors must meet specific eligibility criteria.

1. Age and Weight:
- You must be between 18 and 65 years old.
- You must weigh at least 50 kg (110 lbs).

2. Health and Medical Conditions:
- You must be in good general health and feel well on the day of donation.
- Hemoglobin levels must be at least 12.5 g/dL.
- You cannot donate if you have tested positive for HIV, Hepatitis B, or Hepatitis C.
- Donors with diabetes can donate if their condition is controlled through diet or oral medications (insulin-dependent diabetics are generally deferred).

3. Recent Activities:
- Tattoos or piercings: You must wait 6 months after getting a tattoo or body piercing before donating blood.
- Travel: Travel to areas endemic with malaria or Zika may require a deferral period of 3 to 12 months.
- Surgeries: Major surgeries require a deferral of 6 to 12 months.

4. Frequency:
- Men can donate whole blood every 90 days (3 months).
- Women can donate whole blood every 120 days (4 months).
